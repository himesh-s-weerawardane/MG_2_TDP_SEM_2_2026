PREPROCESSING AND DATA ANALYSIS

Labels: 0 = negative/bearish; 1 = positive/bullish.
Raw TweetEval neutral rows are excluded to align both datasets.
Cleaning: decode HTML, replace URLs/mentions, normalize whitespace,
remove empty text and duplicate text/label rows within each split.
Preserve case, punctuation, emojis, hashtags, tickers and negation.
Tokenization: whitespace tokens for EDA; use the selected BERT
tokenizer during training and fit TF-IDF on training text only.
Mixed training contains only general train + finance train.
Finance validation and test remain separate for model evaluation.
